Complex Numbers

Algebraic form, polar form, multiplication and roots of unity show how numbers live, turn and repeat in the plane.

Algebraic Form

A number is a point in the plane

Real part on the horizontal axis, imaginary part on the vertical axis.

z=a+b i i2=−1 z̄=a−b i

Polar & Euler Form

A length and an angle

The modulus is the distance from 0; the argument is the angle from the positive real axis.

z=r(cos θ+i sin θ)=r eiθ

Multiplication

Multiply lengths, add angles

Multiplying by a complex number scales and rotates. Multiplying by i turns a point 90°.

z1z2=r1r2 ei(θ1+θ2)

Roots of Unity

De Moivre: powers multiply the angle

The n solutions of zn = 1 sit evenly spaced on the unit circle.

(eiθ)n=einθ zk=e2πik/n
